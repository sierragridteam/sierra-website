[CALAVERAS COUNTY, CA] – NOV 10, 2026 — The Signal Integrity & Emergency Radio Response Alliance (S.I.E.R.R.A) 501(c)(3) announced today that it has been awarded a grant from the Calaveras Community Foundation to expand its MeshCore off-grid emergency SMS communications network along the Hwy 4 corridor from Vallecito to Bear Valley, CA.

The funding will directly support the installation of additional solar-powered relay infrastructure along this key transit corridor over the next year, bolstering local resilience and ensuring when critical grid communications fail, SMS communications using a MeshCore off-grid technology remain active during wildfires, severe winter storms, and prolonged power outages.

S.I.E.R.R.A will be announcing their first MeshCore 101 Introductory course later this month which will be free to residents of Calaveras County to learn about this technology. Those interested after the introductory class, may attend a follow up class for those who want hands on instruction to use the network and involve friends and family members.

The network will be free to use and requires a small device users must purchase (one-time no monthly fee) that connects to your cell phone and allows you to send and receive messages with free software installed on your cellphone (Android/Apple both work).

We are a 100% volunteer non-profit public safety organization at https://sierragridteam.org and we appreciate any and all donations which can be made directly through our website at https://sierragridteam.org/donate.
